# Sources — ai-content-factory-brand-consistency

> *Primary sources for the [ai-content-factory-brand-consistency](../SKILL.md) skill.*

## Rules that high-risk content sits under

- **U.S. Federal Trade Commission.** *Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising*, 16 CFR Part 255 (revised 2023). **Primary source for treating endorsements and testimonials as high-risk claims (Step 3).** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255
- **U.S. Federal Trade Commission** (August 14, 2024). "Federal Trade Commission Announces Final Rule Banning Fake Reviews and Testimonials." **Primary source that the final rule covers reviews by someone who does not exist, "such as AI-generated fake reviews."** https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials · Rule text: 16 CFR Part 465, https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-465
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act), Article 50** — transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems, including disclosure of AI-generated or manipulated content. **Primary source for the AI-disclosure check in Step 4.** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- **U.S. Copyright Office.** "Copyright and Artificial Intelligence" (policy studies and reports). **Primary source on the copyright status of AI-generated material.** https://www.copyright.gov/ai/

## Search quality and risk management

- **Google Search Central Blog** (February 8, 2023). "Google Search's guidance about AI-generated content." **Primary source that ranking systems aim at "rewarding high-quality content, however it is produced"** — the reason volume without quality does not compound. https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/02/google-search-and-ai-content
- **Google Search Central.** "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content." **Primary source for the quality criteria the style QA step serves.** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- **NIST.** AI Risk Management Framework. **Primary source for governing generative-AI risk, including confabulation.** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

## Executable voice spec (example)

- **Mailchimp Content Style Guide**, "Voice and Tone." **Public example of a voice spec written as is / is-not pairs and examples rather than adjectives.** https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/

## What is NOT cited and why

- The process itself — voice spec → fact base → risk-tiered gates → measurement loop — is a deciqAI practice synthesis, not a single published framework.
- **Dropped:** an FTC business-blog post, "Keep your AI claims in check" (February 2023), cited in the source draft. Its URL no longer resolves on ftc.gov, so it is not relied on here.
- Rules on endorsements and AI disclosure differ by jurisdiction and change; the sources above show the class of rule, not a complete or current compliance list.
