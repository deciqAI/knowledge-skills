# Sources — ai-roi-business-case

> *Primary sources for the [ai-roi-business-case](../SKILL.md) skill.*

- **NIST (2023).** *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1. **Source for documenting benefits and costs before deployment** — MAP 3.1 ("Potential benefits of intended AI system functionality and performance are examined and documented") and MAP 3.2 ("Potential costs, including non-monetary costs, which result from expected or realized AI errors or system functionality and trustworthiness … are examined and documented"). https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
- **Hubbard, Douglas W. (2014).** *How to Measure Anything: Finding the Value of "Intangibles" in Business*, 3rd ed. Wiley. **Source for measuring baselines and treating uncertain value as quantified ranges** rather than "efficiency gains." https://www.wiley.com/en-us/how-to-measure-anything-finding-the-value-of-intangibles-in-business-3rd-edition-p-9781118539279
- **Duke, Annie (2022).** *Quit: The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away*. Portfolio/Penguin Random House. **Source for pre-committed kill criteria.** https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/692752/quit-by-annie-duke/ · Excerpt on kill criteria: Duke, "Mental Models to Help You Cut Your Losses," *Behavioral Scientist*, November 7, 2022. https://behavioralscientist.org/annie-duke-quit-mental-models-to-help-you-cut-your-losses/
- **Klarna (2024, February 27).** "Klarna AI assistant handles two-thirds of customer service chats in its first month." Press release. **Primary source for the worked example's published case** (2.3M conversations, two-thirds of chats, 700 FTE-equivalent, 11 → under 2 minutes, 25% fewer repeat inquiries, $40M estimated 2024 profit improvement). https://www.klarna.com/international/press/klarna-ai-assistant-handles-two-thirds-of-customer-service-chats-in-its-first-month/
- **Bloomberg (2025, May 8).** "Klarna Slows AI-Driven Job Cuts With Call for Real People." **Primary source for the CEO's quality statement** ("As cost unfortunately seems to have been a too predominant evaluation factor … what you end up having is lower quality"). Paywalled; URL and quote confirmed via search index. https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-08/klarna-turns-from-ai-to-real-person-customer-service
- **Fortune (2025, May 9).** "As Klarna flips from AI-first to hiring people again…" **Open-access corroboration** of the Bloomberg quotes, the return to hiring human agents, and Klarna's "very much still AI-first" clarification. https://fortune.com/2025/05/09/klarna-ai-humans-return-on-investment/

**What is NOT cited and why:** the 8-step sequence (baseline → levers → full cost → adoption ramp → payback → sensitivity → kill line) is a deciqAI synthesis of standard capital-budgeting practice (payback period, scenario analysis) applied to AI projects. No adoption-rate, ROI or inference-price benchmark figures are cited, because the method uses none; a corporate-finance textbook is not listed because no specific claim relies on it.
