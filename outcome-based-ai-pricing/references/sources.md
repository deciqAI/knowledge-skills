# Sources — outcome-based-ai-pricing

> *Primary sources for the [outcome-based-ai-pricing](../SKILL.md) skill.*

- **Ramanujam, M., & Tacke, G.** (2016). *Monetizing Innovation: How Smart Companies Design the Product Around the Price.* Wiley. ISBN 978-1-119-24086-0. **Source for treating price and pricing model as a product-design decision** made before building, including choice of value metric. https://www.wiley.com/en-sg/monetizing-innovation-how-smart-companies-design-the-product-around-the-price-p-9781119240860
- **Nagle, T. T., Müller, G., & Gruyaert, E.** *The Strategy and Tactics of Pricing: A Guide to Growing More Profitably* (7th ed.). Routledge, 2023. **Value-based pricing foundations** (pricing to customer value rather than cost-plus). https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003179566
- **Intercom.** "A complete guide to Fin, the AI bot transforming customer service." Intercom blog, 13 June 2023. **Primary source for the original per-resolution definition and the stated rejection of deflection / API-call pricing.** https://www.intercom.com/blog/fin-ai-bot-customer-service
- **Intercom.** "Fin 2: The first AI agent that delivers human-quality service." Intercom blog, 10 October 2024. **Launch in March 2023 answering "over 25% of customer questions"; price "the same as it always did – $0.99 cents per resolution"; average resolution rate 51%.** https://www.intercom.com/blog/announcing-fin-2-ai-agent-customer-service
- **Intercom Help Center.** "Fin AI Agent outcomes." **Current billing definitions**: confirmed vs. assumed resolution; once-per-conversation charge; unbilled escalations; deduction when a resolved conversation is reopened; usage reminders and hard limits; $9.99 qualification outcome. https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205718-fin-ai-agent-outcomes
- **Intercom.** Pricing page. **Seats + usage structure; $0.99 per outcome; minimum monthly commitment for standalone Fin (e.g. 50 outcomes); 14-day trial.** https://www.intercom.com/pricing
- **Salesforce.** Agentforce pricing. **Contrasting public example of usage pricing for AI agents** (per-conversation pricing and Flex Credits per action, alongside per-user add-ons). https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/
- **U.S. Department of Energy, Federal Energy Management Program.** "Energy Savings Performance Contracts for Federal Agencies." **Long-established outcome-based contracting**: an energy service company funds improvements and the agency repays from the resulting energy-cost savings, with guaranteed savings and terms up to 25 years. https://www.energy.gov/femp/energy-savings-performance-contracts-federal-agencies
- **Anthropic** and **OpenAI** API pricing pages — inference-cost inputs for step 4 (per-token prices change with model releases; check live before use). https://claude.com/pricing · https://openai.com/api/pricing/

## What is NOT cited and why

- The four-test scoring of charge units, the P50/P90 margin check, and the migration sequence (new customers → opt-in → switch at renewal) are **deciqAI practice synthesis**, not a published standard.
- Vendor price pages are cited as public examples only; prices and definitions change, and Intercom's per-resolution margin and inference cost are not public.
