# Sources — agent-ops-monitoring

> *Primary sources for the [agent-ops-monitoring](../SKILL.md) skill. All URLs checked October 2026.*

- **Beyer, B., Jones, C., Petoff, J., & Murphy, N. R. (Eds.) (2016). *Site Reliability Engineering*. O'Reilly / Google.** Ch. 6, "Monitoring Distributed Systems": "Your monitoring system should address two questions: what's broken, and why?", the symptom-vs-cause distinction and the four golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, saturation). https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/ · Ch. 15, "Postmortem Culture: Learning from Failure" (blameless postmortems). https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/
- **OpenTelemetry. GenAI semantic conventions.** Standard attribute names for model, token usage, agent and tool-call spans. The conventions now live in their own repository, and the former page at opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/gen-ai/ is marked "Moved." https://github.com/open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai
- **Anthropic (2024). "Building effective agents."** Agents' autonomy brings "higher costs, and the potential for compounding errors," and Anthropic recommends "extensive testing in sandboxed environments, along with the appropriate guardrails" and human checkpoints. https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents
- **NIST (2023). *AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1.** The MEASURE and MANAGE functions cover ongoing monitoring of deployed AI systems and incident response. https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
- **OpenAI (April 29, 2025). "Sycophancy in GPT-4o: What happened and what we're doing about it."** Primary source for the case study: the update was rolled back because it was "overly flattering or agreeable." https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/
- **OpenAI (May 2, 2025). "Expanding on what we missed with sycophancy."** Primary source for the case-study timeline (rollout April 24–25, mitigation Sunday, full rollback starting Monday, about 24 hours), for offline evals and A/B tests looking good, for expert testers' "felt off" signal being overridden, and for the process changes. https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy/

**What is NOT cited and why:** the specific field list, alert set and weekly-review agenda are deciqAI's synthesis of SRE practice adapted to LLM agents. No numeric thresholds are prescribed, because they depend on each workload's baseline. The skill tells the running agent to derive thresholds from the workload's own data, not from memory. "Most errors are never reported" is a designed [D] heuristic, not a cited statistic.
