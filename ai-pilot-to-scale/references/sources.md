# Sources — ai-pilot-to-scale

> *Primary sources for the [ai-pilot-to-scale](../SKILL.md) skill.*

- **Gartner (2024, July 29).** "Gartner Predicts 30% of Generative AI Projects Will Be Abandoned After Proof of Concept By End of 2025." Press release. **Source for the pilot-purgatory industry signal** — "at least 30%" abandoned after POC, "due to poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs or unclear business value." https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-07-29-gartner-predicts-30-percent-of-generative-ai-projects-will-be-abandoned-after-proof-of-concept-by-end-of-2025
- **Zinkevich, Martin (Google).** "Rules of Machine Learning: Best Practices for ML Engineering." Google for Developers. **Source for metrics-before-model** — Rule #1 "Don't be afraid to launch a product without machine learning"; Rule #2 "First, design and implement metrics." https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/guides/rules-of-ml
- **Anthropic (2024, December).** "Building effective agents." **Source for staged autonomy** — "we recommend finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity when needed. This might mean not building agentic systems at all." https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents
- **Brynjolfsson, Erik; Li, Danielle; Raymond, Lindsey R.** "Generative AI at Work." NBER Working Paper 31161, April 2023 (revised November 2023). **Primary source for the worked example**: seven-week randomized pilot with 50 agents, staged rollout (bulk November 2020–February 2021), suggest-only design inside the existing chat window, +14% resolutions per hour on average, +34% for novice/low-skilled agents, 38% average adherence. https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161 · Published version: *The Quarterly Journal of Economics* 140(2), 2025. https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658

**What is NOT cited and why:** the 2-week minimum baseline, the ~8-week decision window, the "revise only once" rule, the 2-department scope gate and the L1/L2/L3 autonomy ladder are deciqAI synthesis for small and mid-size teams, not a published standard. Books on pre-committed pivot decisions (Ries, *The Lean Startup*) and constraint selection (Goldratt, *The Goal*) inform the method but are not relied on for any factual claim, so they are not listed as sources.
