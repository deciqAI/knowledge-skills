# Sources — build-buy-partner-ai

> *Primary sources for the [build-buy-partner-ai](../SKILL.md) skill.*

- **Sculley, D., et al. (2015).** "Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems." *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 28 (NeurIPS 2015).* **Source for counting maintenance, not just build, in TCO** — "it is common to incur massive ongoing maintenance costs in real-world ML systems." https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2015/hash/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Abstract.html
- **Prahalad, C. K., & Hamel, Gary (1990).** "The Core Competence of the Corporation." *Harvard Business Review*, May–June 1990. **Source for the differentiation test** — keep in-house what differentiates. https://hbr.org/1990/05/the-core-competence-of-the-corporation
- **Shapiro, Carl, & Varian, Hal R. (1998).** *Information Rules: A Strategic Guide to the Network Economy.* Harvard Business School Press. **Source for the lock-in and switching-cost assessment** (step 6). Catalog record: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/578912
- **NIST (2023).** *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1. **Source for third-party/vendor AI risk** — GOVERN 6.1 (policies addressing "AI risks associated with third-party entities") and MANAGE 3.1–3.2 (third-party risks monitored; pre-trained models monitored as part of maintenance). https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
- **Wu, Shijie, et al. (2023).** "BloombergGPT: A Large Language Model for Finance." arXiv:2303.17564 (submitted March 30, 2023; revised December 21, 2023). **Primary source for the worked example**: 50B parameters; 363B-token proprietary FinPile plus 345B general tokens; 1.3M A100 GPU-hour compute budget; 512 A100s; ~53 days of training; FinPile not releasable. https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17564
- **OpenAI (2023).** "GPT-4 Technical Report." arXiv:2303.08774 (submitted March 15, 2023). **Source for the general-model release date** in the worked example. https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08774
- **Li, Xianzhi, et al. (2023).** "Are ChatGPT and GPT-4 General-Purpose Solvers for Financial Text Analytics? A Study on Several Typical Tasks." arXiv:2305.05862 (submitted May 10, 2023; revised October 10, 2023). **Source for the outside comparison**: GPT-4 better than BloombergGPT (reported numbers) on FiQA sentiment and news-headline classification; general models "still fall short on others, particularly when deeper semantics and structural analysis are needed." https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05862

**What is NOT cited and why:** the decision-matrix dimensions, the "~3× time-to-value" heuristic and the "re-run on every major model release" trigger are deciqAI synthesis for small and mid-size teams, not a published standard. The claim that foundation models are superseded quickly is illustrated by the worked example (two weeks between GPT-4 and BloombergGPT's publication) rather than by a cited release-cadence figure; no cadence number is relied on.
