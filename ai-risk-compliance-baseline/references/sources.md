# Sources — ai-risk-compliance-baseline

> *Primary sources for the [ai-risk-compliance-baseline](../SKILL.md) skill.*

> **Currency note.** Every instrument below is amended, supplemented by guidance, or phased in over time. This file deliberately records *what each source is used for*, not any threshold, fine, tier or date from it. Read the current version before relying on any of them.

**Frameworks used as comparison checklists**

- **NIST (2023).** *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1. **Source for the inventory step** (GOVERN 1.6: "Mechanisms are in place to inventory AI systems…") and **third-party/vendor risk** (GOVERN 6.1; MANAGE 3.1). Landing page (notes the framework is under revision): https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework · Full text: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
- **NIST (2024, July).** *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile*, NIST AI 600-1. **Source for generative-AI-specific risks**, including direct and indirect prompt injection. https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf
- **OWASP GenAI Security Project.** *Top 10 for LLM Applications* (2025 edition: LLM01 Prompt Injection, LLM02 Sensitive Information Disclosure, LLM05 Improper Output Handling, LLM06 Excessive Agency). **Source for the security item.** https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023.** *Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system.* **Reference for what a full AI management system covers**, against which this baseline is a deliberate minimum. https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html

**Law and regulator guidance (named as instruments to check, not summarized)**

- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act).** Article 50 — transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems. **Source for the disclosure item.** Official text (ELI; EUR-Lex presents an automated-access challenge, so verified via the mirror below): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj · Article 50 text, verified: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/
- **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).** **Named as an example privacy regime for the privacy item.** Official text (ELI; same EUR-Lex access challenge — the identifier is the official one): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- **U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright and Artificial Intelligence.** Multi-part report (digital replicas; copyrightability of generative-AI outputs; training). **Source for the IP item's "check the copyright authority's current position."** https://www.copyright.gov/ai/
- **U.S. FTC (2024, September 25).** "FTC Announces Crackdown on Deceptive AI Claims and Schemes" (Operation AI Comply). **Source for "do not overstate AI capability"** — "there is no AI exemption from the laws on the books." https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-announces-crackdown-deceptive-ai-claims-schemes
- **U.S. FTC Business Blog — Atleson, Michael (2023, February).** "Keep your AI claims in check." **Corroborating source for the claims item** ("you can't just blame a third-party developer of the technology"). The original URL (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check) returned 404 when checked; archived copy verified: https://web.archive.org/web/20250317232347/https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check

**Worked example**

- **Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149** (B.C. Civil Resolution Tribunal, February 14, 2024). **Primary source for the worked example.** https://decisions.civilresolutionbc.ca/crt/crtd/en/item/525448/index.do
- **CBC News, Jason Proctor (2024, February 15).** "Air Canada found liable for chatbot's bad advice on plane tickets." **Verbatim quotations from the decision** used in the example. https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/air-canada-chatbot-lawsuit-1.7116416
- **American Bar Association, Business Law Today — Lifshitz & Hung (2024, February 29).** "BC Tribunal Confirms Companies Remain Liable for Information Provided by AI Chatbot." **Corroborating summary.** https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/business-law-today/2024-february/bc-tribunal-confirms-companies-remain-liable-information-provided-ai-chatbot/

**What is NOT cited and why:** the 7-item baseline, the green/amber/red marking and the "pause until amber" gate are a deciqAI synthesis for small teams drawn from the frameworks above. It is baseline practice, not a legal determination for any jurisdiction, and no threshold, fine amount, risk tier, applicability test or effective date from any of these instruments is reproduced here — by design.
