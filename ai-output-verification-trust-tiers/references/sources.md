# Sources — ai-output-verification-trust-tiers

> *Primary sources for the [ai-output-verification-trust-tiers](../SKILL.md) skill.*

## Risk frameworks

- **NIST** (2024). *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile*, NIST AI 600-1. **Primary source for "confabulation" — "the production of confidently stated but erroneous or false content" — as a named generative-AI risk, and for automation bias / over-reliance as a human-AI configuration risk.** https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf
- **NIST** (2023). *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1. **Primary source for the govern / map / measure / manage structure the tier table operationalizes.** https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act), Article 14 — Human oversight.** **Primary source for designing AI systems so they "can be effectively overseen by natural persons," including awareness of automation bias.** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj

## Human reviewers

- **Parasuraman, R. & Manzey, D. H.** (2010). "Complacency and Bias in Human Use of Automation: An Attentional Integration." *Human Factors*, 52(3), 381–410. **Primary source for automation complacency and automation bias — why reviewers of reliable-seeming systems start approving without checking** (Applying It Well #4; the "review everything" rationalization). https://doi.org/10.1177/0018720810376055

## Worked example

- ***Mata v. Avianca, Inc.***, No. 22-cv-1461 (PKC), Opinion and Order on Sanctions (S.D.N.Y. June 22, 2023), ECF 54; reported at 678 F. Supp. 3d 443. **Primary source for every fact in the example: the fabricated opinions, the style-only review, the screenshots of counsel asking ChatGPT whether the cases were real, the ignored challenges, and the sanctions.** https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/new-york/nysdce/1:2022cv01461/575368/54/ · Docket: https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/63107798/mata-v-avianca-inc/

## What is NOT cited and why

- The **T1–T4 tiering by error cost × verifiability**, the evidence rule ("no source = unverified") and the re-tiering rule are a deciqAI synthesis, not a published standard.
- **No AI accuracy or hallucination-rate statistics are cited.** Published rates vary by model, task and date, and the skill deliberately tiers by error cost rather than by any model's average accuracy.
