# Sources — agent-outreach-lead-nurturing

> *Primary sources for the [agent-outreach-lead-nurturing](../SKILL.md) skill. All URLs checked October 2026. Thresholds and penalty amounts on these pages change. Read them live and never quote them from memory.*

- **U.S. Federal Trade Commission. "CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business."** Covers all commercial email and "makes no exception for business-to-business email." Requirements: accurate header information, non-deceptive subject lines, identification as an ad, a valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out honored promptly, and responsibility that cannot be contracted away when others send on your behalf. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- **U.S. Federal Trade Commission (August 30, 2024). Verkada press release.** Primary source for the case study: more than 30 million commercial emails over three years; alleged failure to include an opt-out, honor opt-outs and provide a postal address; $2.95 million penalty, "the largest penalty obtained by the FTC for a CAN-SPAM violation." https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/ftc-takes-action-against-security-camera-firm-verkada-over-charges-it-failed-secure-videos-other
- **Verkada (August 30, 2024). "FTC Settlement: Explained."** The company's response: emails to businesses and organizations 2019–2021; disagreement with the allegations; stated remediation (preference page, standardized footer with physical address). https://www.verkada.com/blog/ftc-settlement-explained/
- **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).** Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interests as a lawful basis; Art. 21(2)–(3) right to object to direct marketing "at any time," after which the data "shall no longer be processed for such purposes." Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj (EUR-Lex blocks automated fetches; article text verified via the mirrors https://gdpr-info.eu/art-6-gdpr/ and https://gdpr-info.eu/art-21-gdpr/).
- **UK Information Commissioner's Office. Direct marketing and PECR guidance.** https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/ · "Electronic mail marketing": sole traders and some partnerships are treated as individuals; corporate bodies may be emailed, but named corporate addresses raise data-protection implications, and a "do not email" list is good practice. https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/
- **Google Workspace Admin Help. "Email sender guidelines."** Authentication and DMARC alignment; marketing and subscribed messages "must support one-click unsubscribe, and include a clearly visible unsubscribe link"; spam-rate ceilings in Postmaster Tools and additional bulk-sender requirements (read current values on the page). https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126
- **Yahoo Sender Hub. "Sender Best Practices."** Bulk-sender requirements: SPF and DKIM, a valid DMARC policy, and one-click unsubscribe via the List-Unsubscribe header (RFC 8058 POST method recommended). https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/
- **IETF RFC 8058. "Signaling One-Click Functionality for List Email Headers."** Defines the List-Unsubscribe-Post header. https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8058
- **IETF RFC 7489. "Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance (DMARC)."** https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7489

**What is NOT cited and why:** trigger signals, the 3–5-touch sequence and the declining spot-check rate are practitioner synthesis, not a published standard. No reply-rate benchmarks are cited. Specific numeric thresholds (spam-rate ceilings, bulk-sender volumes, per-email penalty maximums, opt-out processing deadlines) are deliberately left out of SKILL.md because they change. The skill tells the running agent to read them from the live pages above.
